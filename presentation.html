The operating principle

Never let supply sit idle

Most marketplaces die because supply churns in the first 30 days. Two simple rules avoid that.

Rule 1, doctors

Stagger onboarding, never list 30 at once

  • 1
    Week 1, onboard 5 doctors only. Run ALL demand ads pointing to these 5. Each gets 8 to 12 bookings.
  • 2
    Week 2, add 5 more. First 5 refer colleagues. Word spreads.
  • 3
    Week 3-4, scale to 20-25. Real volume exists. New doctors see proof before they start.

Plus: dashboard views ("Your profile was viewed 34 times this week") and a 10-consult guarantee in Month 1, or zero commission Month 2.

Rule 2, gig workers

Don't mass recruit until demand exists

  • 1
    Month 1, 10-15 testers only. Finalize SOP, vetting.
  • 2
    Month 2, registration campaign. Phase 2 launches Month 3-4. 6-8 weeks lead time is enough.
  • 3
    Month 3-4, full activation. Workers get bookings within days of joining.

Founding Crew: exclusive WhatsApp group, weekly progress updates, micro-tasks (SOP videos), 0% commission for first 50 workers' first month.

Why this works
The doctors you know personally, use those relationships to onboard the first 5. Once those 5 start getting bookings, every other doctor you approach already knows it works. Your personal network solves the cold start. That is why the founding doctors' network is the single most valuable non-monetary asset in this business right now.
